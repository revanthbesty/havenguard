HavenGuard — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

HavenGuard is designed to help parents restrict selected known unsafe websites. It cannot guarantee complete protection, and an unmanaged browser extension can be disabled or removed.

Information stored locally

HavenGuard stores parent protection settings, website allow/block lists, child profile settings, family routines, approximate website time-limit counters, a salted derived parent-PIN verifier, and category-level safety events in the browser's extension storage. The parent PIN is not stored as plaintext. Temporary parent-unlock state is kept in session storage. A parent can export a settings backup to a file they control.

Browsing and download activity

To enforce the selected protections, HavenGuard processes relevant browsing and download URLs locally. Its category-level safety event records do not include full visited URLs. HavenGuard cannot monitor every website, browser profile, app, or device.

Data transmission and third parties

In the reviewed HavenGuard v1.21.0 build, there is no developer-operated telemetry upload or analytics service. Optional Google/Bing search-safety and YouTube restriction settings can modify requests sent directly to those services by the browser. If you follow an external help link, that website's own privacy policy applies. HavenGuard does not sell locally stored family settings or browsing activity to advertisers. Future versions that change data handling will require an updated disclosure.

Parent controls and deletion

Parents can review or delete local activity, export backups, and reset HavenGuard from its settings. Removing the extension generally removes its browser-profile extension storage; backups exported separately remain in their saved location and must be deleted separately.

Security and limitations

Parent PIN authorization restricts changes within HavenGuard. It does not prevent uninstalling or disabling an unmanaged extension. Parents should also use standard Windows child accounts, operating-system parental controls, and appropriate supervision. No website-blocking solution is guaranteed to identify every harmful destination.

Children

HavenGuard is intended to be configured by a parent or guardian. Children do not need to provide their name, email address, or other identifying details to browse using the extension. Family activity data is stored locally as described above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when HavenGuard's features or data-handling practices change. The effective date will be revised when that happens.

Questions and support

For privacy questions or support, please use the project's GitHub Issues page. Please do not post PINs, private browsing history, or other sensitive information in public issues.
